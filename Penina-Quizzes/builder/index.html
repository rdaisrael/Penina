Download quiz vocabulary template (CSV)

Fill in Word and Definition, one pair per row. Replace the example rows. This is a simpler template than the Vocab Builder workbook. For an Excel workbook, copy its two relevant columns and paste below, or export them as CSV.

Use one word–definition pair per line. Separate the two columns with a comma (שלום,peace) or a tab (copy two columns from Excel or Google Sheets). A two-column Markdown table also works. In CSV, put definitions containing commas in quotation marks: שלום,"peace, hello". Semicolons and dashes are not column separators. Excel workbook uploads are not supported here.

Question types to include

Pictured knowledge questions use written answer, multiple choice, multiple answers, or fill-in-the-blanks. Matching uses questions without pictures; alphabetical ordering uses vocabulary only. Formats with no eligible items are skipped and reported.

Multiple answers asks about one term with several correct answers. Enter those under Additional correct answers, one per line; distractors are incorrect choices. Questions are spread evenly across the selected types. A matching group contains 4–6 terms and counts as one question. The suggested count covers as many untested words as these formats allow, without repeating a word. Change formats if some words remain untested. Ordering questions ask for alphabetical order.
