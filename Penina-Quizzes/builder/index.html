Generate a quiz from vocabulary

Upload 10–15 words and definitions, review the suggested distractors, then choose your quiz. Lists of 2–50 words are supported.

1. Add your vocabulary

CSV, tab-separated text, or a Markdown table with two columns: word and definition. Paste a table from chat, or copy two columns from Excel or Google Sheets.

2. Review words and distractors

For Hebrew terms, enter English translations and distractors. For English terms, enter Hebrew translations and distractors. All answer choices use that opposite language. Edit any cell; put one distractor on each line. Generated questions use only the distractors in that term’s row. Matching includes paired terms and definitions, plus distractors.

Question types to include

Multiple answers asks about one term with several correct answers. Enter those under Additional correct answers, one per line; distractors are incorrect choices. Questions are spread evenly across the selected types. A matching group counts as one question. Every word is covered before any word is reused, including words in groups. You will be warned before generating a quiz that needs repeats. Ordering questions ask for alphabetical order.
