Gemara 6 WL - Intro (Retake)

Assign to a Google Classroom class

  1. Your teacher account connects to Google Classroom.
  2. Choose the class whose students may access this quiz.
  3. Create the Classroom draft, review it, then publish it to students.

Students sign in with Google. Only students currently enrolled in the selected class can submit student work. Class teachers can take practice attempts.

Open assignment in Classroom

Each class needs its own quiz link. Use Correct quizzes from Teacher home to review student work after assigning.

Review and grade in Penina

Your teacher account gives you access to these submissions. This quiz has 6 questions worth 8 points. Questions 2–5 are graded automatically using the original BookWidgets answer key. Enter 0–2 points for question 1 and 0–1 point for question 6, then save your corrections or send the completed grade to Classroom.
