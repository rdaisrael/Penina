Assign in Google Classroom

  1. Click Choose Google account & class below. Google asks which account you want to use. Choose your school account, or select Use another account.
  2. In Classroom, choose a class from Share to class, then click Go to next step.
  3. Finish the assignment title, instructions, points, and due date in Classroom, then assign it.

Students open the quiz from that assignment, submit their answers in Penina, then return to Classroom and click Turn in.

Choose Google account & class

This shares the quiz link with the class you select. Student answers and corrections are saved in Penina. Enter final grades in Classroom manually.

Review and grade in Penina

Use your Sixth Grade vocabulary publishing password. Questions 1–12 are worth 1 point each. Questions 13–14 allow 4 extra-credit points. The 10 multiple-choice questions are graded automatically using the original BookWidgets answer key. Open each submission to enter points for questions 3, 6, 13, and 14, then save your corrections.
