Assign to a Google Classroom class

  1. Enter your teacher password below and connect your Google teacher account.
  2. Choose the class whose students may access this quiz.
  3. Create the Classroom draft, then open Classroom to review and assign it.

Students sign in with Google. Only students currently enrolled in the selected class can submit student work. Class teachers can take practice attempts.

Open assignment in Classroom

Answers and corrections are saved in Penina. After correcting questions 1 and 6, use “Save and send grade to Classroom.” Students must mark the assignment done in Classroom before it can be returned. Each class needs its own quiz link.

Review and grade in Penina

Use your Sixth Grade vocabulary publishing password. This quiz has 6 questions worth 8 points. Questions 2–5 are graded automatically using the original BookWidgets answer key. Enter 0–2 points for question 1 and 0–1 point for question 6, then save your corrections or send the completed grade to Classroom.
