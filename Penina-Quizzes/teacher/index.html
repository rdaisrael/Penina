Edit Potential Points

Changes apply to every class using this quiz, before or after submission or grading. Earned points stay unchanged: 2/4 becomes 2/5. The original answer-key awards also stay unchanged for future submissions.

Extra-credit questions remain separate from the regular quiz total. If the total changes, update the assignment’s points possible in Google Classroom before sending revised grades.

No emails are sent here. Review the adjusted grades, save and send them to Classroom, then use Send Email to send updated copies to students.
