Each retained entry includes its Wiktionary source URL. Contributor histories
are available through the History tab of those entries. No quotations, example
sentences, audio, images, or other separately licensed illustrations are used.

Changes: selected verb entries with complete pointed paradigms; excluded senses
marked archaic, rare, Biblical-only and other unsuitable registers; normalized
root notation; supplied root/binyan identities for several explicit irregular
tables; retained the first listed pointed form per cell and classroom shared
feminine-plural future/imperative forms; classified each tense against a named
reference pattern; kept source meanings and individual forms. Missing or
incomplete paradigms are omitted. This is not a complete or independently
reviewed inventory of Modern Hebrew verbs.
