    Additional Information

    Paper Flashcards

    Print double-sided, flip on the short edge, use 100% scale, and turn off headers and footers. Cut along the dotted lines.

    Create Distractors for Practice Activities

    Prepare four incorrect answers for each question. Generate distractors in English or Hebrew, then review, edit, or regenerate them. You can also enter your own.

    The correct answer stays in the language you entered. Editing it here updates the flashcard. Leave all four distractors empty to include a question only in flashcards and the knowledge sheet.
