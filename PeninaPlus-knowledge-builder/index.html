Import a Spreadsheet

Download the blank template and add one question per row. Enter the answer text in Correct Answer, followed by any incorrect answers in Distractor 1–3. Leave distractors blank to generate them after uploading. Pictures and Additional Information are optional.

Download Excel Template

Insert each picture in its Picture cell, or position it over that cell. Upload up to 500 questions. Existing A–F sheets with a Correct Answer letter are also supported. Review and edit imported cards before sharing.

    Additional Information

    Paper Flashcards

    Print double-sided, flip on the short edge, use 100% scale, and turn off headers and footers. Cut along the dotted lines.

    Create Distractors for Practice Activities

    Prepare incorrect answers for each question. New questions start with three distractors, for four choices in Penina Live and Quiz. Imported questions keep their supplied choices, up to five distractors. Generate answers in English or Hebrew, then review, edit, or regenerate them.

    The correct answer stays in the language you entered. Editing it here updates the flashcard. Leave all distractors empty to include a question only in flashcards and the knowledge sheet.
