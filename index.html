My videos

To add a video, sign in with David Israel’s account using Teacher sign-in in the upper right. This My videos panel appears below the app cards.

  1. Enter a title and choose an MP4 video up to 1 GB. H.264 video with AAC audio works in the widest range of browsers.
  2. Click Upload video and keep this page open until it finishes.
  3. The new viewer URL will appear in this My videos list and automatically on the public video library.
  4. Click the clipboard icon beside the viewer URL to copy the standalone page, then paste it into a Google Classroom assignment.

Anyone who can access the library or viewer URL can watch its videos.
